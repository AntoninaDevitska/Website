The most effective educators are not the ones who never tire,
they are the ones who know how to restore.

BREATHE Teaching & Wellbeing Forum is a transformative three-day retreat designed exclusively for educators who refuse to choose between professional excellence and personal wellbeing.

Set in the breathtaking Carpathian Mountains, BREATHE brings together world-class pedagogical sessions, evidence-based restoration practices, and an international community of like-minded professionals, because your impact in the classroom begins with the quality of your inner life.

The BREATHE Experience

Three transformative days.
One defining experience.

World-Class Pedagogical Sessions

Expert speakers from EdYOUFest, TESOL & IATEFL International bring cutting-edge insights in AI in Education, SEL, Drama in EFL, and Digital Storytelling, with actionable strategies you can implement from day one back in your classroom.

Evidence-Based Wellbeing Practices

Led by a certified psychologist, these sessions go beyond theory: embodiment work, creative expression, and self-regulation techniques that produce measurable shifts in resilience, clarity and emotional capacity.

Health & Sustainable Performance

A health coach delivers practical, science-informed frameworks for energy management and longevity, built specifically for the demands educators face, without the overwhelm of complex lifestyle overhauls.

Creative Practices That Transform

Drama, Visual Emotion Exploration Workshop and storytelling, experienced both as powerful teaching methodologies and as profound tools for personal renewal. These are the sessions participants return to talk about most.

An International Professional Community

Connect with educators and educational leaders from across borders. Build relationships with peers who share your values, and gain access to partnerships, collaborations and opportunities you won't find anywhere else.

A Setting That Accelerates Restoration

The Carpathian Mountains provide more than a backdrop, they are integral to the experience. SPA, a lakeside retreat space, mountain air and natural stillness form an environment where deep restoration happens naturally.

The Structure of Transformation

Intentionally designed.
Deeply felt.

Every element of BREATHE has been purposefully crafted, not simply to fill your schedule, but to create genuine shifts in how you think, feel and move through your work. Each day follows a rhythm that honours both your professional ambition and your human need to restore.

🌅Mornings open with grounding rituals by the lake, setting clear intention for the day ahead
🎓Days are dedicated to high-impact sessions, workshops and collaborative professional exchange
🌙Evenings dissolve into lakeside meditation, meaningful conversation and restorative SPA time
🏔️The Carpathian landscape, with its ancient forests, mountain air and natural silence, supports renewal at every level
🤫A dedicated quiet space is available throughout for reflection, solitude and deep inner work
